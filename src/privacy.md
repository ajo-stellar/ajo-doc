# Privacy

Public member/admin/token addresses, contribution amounts, creation timestamps, payout order, rounds, contribution flags and accounting totals are stored on-chain. Events expose contribution and payout activity. Addresses can be linked to people.

Never enter names, phone numbers, emails or IDs. The app has no personal-data fields, trackers or backend. Wallet icons are local. RPC and chosen wallets may observe their own network requests. Never share seed phrases.
